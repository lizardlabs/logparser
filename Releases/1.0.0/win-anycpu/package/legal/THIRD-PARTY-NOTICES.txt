Universal Log Parser third-party notices
========================================

license texts are located under:

  docs/Documentation/UserGuide/legal/

  docs/Documentation/UserGuide/legal/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md

license itself has not yet been supplied as an approved root LICENSE file.
